Our mission

No girl should miss school, compromise her comfort or sacrifice her dignity because she cannot afford menstrual hygiene products.

Amaris is a period-care and social-impact brand. It combines entrepreneurship, menstrual health advocacy and social responsibility, and it exists to help girls who cannot afford sanitary products and to fight period poverty.

Our impact

1,000+sanitary products donated to girls in need

Through community donations and outreach initiatives, Amaris puts essential products directly into the hands of the girls who need them.

Our story

  1. March 28, 2025

  2. Mission takes shape

  3. 2026

    The Luxe Shoppe is rebranded as Amaris.
